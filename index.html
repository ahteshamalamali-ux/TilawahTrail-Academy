Helpful answers

Questions, answered.

Still deciding? Ask us about what matters to you.

Can a complete beginner start?

Yes. Qaida and Arabic letters are a natural starting point for someone new to reading Arabic.

Are classes for adults and children?

The learning paths shown here welcome both children and adults. Ask about a suitable pace and lesson format.

How do I choose a course?

Describe the learner’s age, experience and goal in the enquiry form. Choose “Not sure yet” if you would like guidance.

How do I ask about fees and timings?

The monthly fees are listed with each course. Use WhatsApp to confirm lesson timings and availability before you decide.

Will the enquiry send automatically?

No. WhatsApp opens with your message ready to review. You decide whether to send it.
